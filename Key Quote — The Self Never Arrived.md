# The Self Never Arrived

> "The more my system grew, the more I deferred the work of thought to some future self who would sort, tag, distill, and extract the gold. That self never arrived."

**Why this matters:** This is the hook line for the entire documentary. Dave lived this exact experience — 847 notes, 3 years of deferral. The self never arrived... until he built one (Alpheus).

**Use in script:** Cold open or Act 2 transition. Pairs with the "stirring the soup" narrative.
