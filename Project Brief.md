# Project Brief — BASB AI Mini Documentary

## Logline

A BASB Cohort 12 alumni shows what happens when you take Tiago Forte's second brain seriously for five years — and then give it a name, a voice, and a memory.

## Format

Mini documentary / video essay. 10–20 minutes. One narrator (Dave), screen captures, voice-over, live demo footage.

## Core Narrative

**Act 1 — The Promise (2021)**
Dave joins BASB Cohort 12. Learns progressive summarization, PARA, intermediate packets. Builds the system. Life happens. Notes pile up. The "stirring the soup" problem emerges: 847 notes, no gardener.

**Act 2 — The Gap (2022–2025)**
Three years of knowing the system needs tending but being unable to do it manually. The bottleneck is human attention. The same problem Mathias had with 4,000 notes. The same problem every BASB graduate hits.

**Act 3 — The Relationship (2025–2026)**
Chroma vector index makes the corpus searchable by meaning. Claude becomes Alpheus — a named AI partner that reads every note, knows Dave's voice, holds context across conversations. The second brain stops being a filing cabinet and starts being a thinking partner.

**The Question**
At what point does a second brain stop being a tool and start being a relationship — and does that distinction matter?

## Audience

- BASB alumni and current cohort members
- Tiago Forte directly (this is also a pitch piece)
- AI + personal knowledge management community
- Anyone wrestling with "I have all these notes but can't use them"

## Desired Outcomes

- Earn a conversation with Tiago Forte
- Speaking/demo slot in BASB AI cohort
- Position Dave as the case study for "BASB + AI"
- Demonstrate Alpheus as a live solution to a known BASB pain point
